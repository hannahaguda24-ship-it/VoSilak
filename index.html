How Vosilak Works

Turn plastic bottles into charging time in six simple steps.

  1. 1
    Insert a Bottle

    Select a port and click Insert Bottle. Follow the timer and click Done Inserting Bottle after your bottle is accepted.

  2. 2
    Earn Charging Time

    Every accepted bottle adds 3 minutes to the selected port.

  3. 3
    Start Charging

    Connect your device to the port and click Start Charging.

  4. 4
    Finish Charging

    Click Finish Charging when you're done. Unused minutes automatically generate a voucher code.

  5. 5
    Redeem Your Voucher

    Click Redeem Voucher and enter the generated code.

  6. 6
    Choose Your Reward

    Convert unused minutes into drinking water or save them for another session on the original port.
